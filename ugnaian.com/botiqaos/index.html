§ 01 · The operator reality

Running a pharmacy shouldn’t be this hard

Pharmacists are trained to care for people, not to fight spreadsheets. Most independent botikas still run on tools never built for them, and the cost shows up as stockouts, expired stock, and decisions made in the dark.

01 · Inventory

Manual counts, hidden stockouts

Notebook ledgers and disconnected POS terminals make it hard to know what’s actually on the shelf, until a customer walks away empty-handed.

02 · Expiry

Expired medicines, written off

Without lot and expiry visibility, slow-moving SKUs age out quietly, turning working capital into a loss line nobody wants to read.

03 · Branches

Branches that don’t talk

One branch is overstocked, another is dry. Without a single source of truth, owners reconcile by phone call, and reconcile late.

04 · Reports

Numbers you can’t trust

Manual entry creates manual errors. Most owners can’t get a clean answer to “which SKUs made money this month” without losing a weekend.

05 · Suppliers

Reorders done from memory

Purchasing becomes guesswork: over-ordering on intuition, under-ordering on hope. The data to do better exists; it’s just out of reach.

06 · Future

Locked out of digital healthcare

E-prescriptions, patient analytics, supplier integrations. Every modern capability needs infrastructure independent pharmacies don’t have yet.

§ 05 · Security & privacy

Built with privacy and compliance in mind

Pharmacy data is sensitive: customer records, prescriptions, financials. We treat it that way from day one, with infrastructure and workflows that respect the Data Privacy Act and the trust your customers place in you.

Data Privacy Act aware by design

Consent flows, retention policies, and data subject rights are handled at the workflow level.

Secure cloud architecture

Encrypted in transit and at rest, Your pharmacy’s data is kept entirely separate from every other pharmacy on the platform, with continuous monitoring.

Role-based access control

Granular permissions so each role sees what it needs, and nothing more.

Audit-friendly workflows

Every transaction, adjustment, and access event is logged, giving clean trails for compliance reviews.
